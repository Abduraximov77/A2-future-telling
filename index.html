A neighborhood internet · Est. 2041

Someone left the light on for you.

Porchlight is a small corner of the internet that works like a front porch: slow, warm, and local. No feeds to scroll. No ads. Just the people who live near you, and the things you can learn from each other.

Walk up to the porch

What it sounds, feels, and looks like

Sounds

Crickets, a screen door, a soft chime when a neighbor says hello. Hello arrives in the language that person speaks at home.

Feels

Unhurried. Posts rest on the porch for a week, and nothing counts your likes. You leave feeling more known, not more tired.

Looks

Lamp-light amber against a deep evening sky. Big, readable type. Rounded corners, like the edges of things you'd want to hold.

Who it's for

Porchlight is built for the people the internet forgets to design for: elders, new arrivals, teenagers who know more than anyone credits, and anyone whose knowledge doesn't fit on a résumé.

Come up the steps. Someone is telling a story.
