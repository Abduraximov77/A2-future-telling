How a trade works

  1. Light a lantern

    Post what you can offer or what you need, in your own words.

  2. Meet in person

    Porchlight only makes the introduction. The real thing happens face to face.

  3. Trade hours

    Give an hour, earn an hour. Spend it with anyone on the street.

What the designer promises

Someone decides who gets to belong in a space like this. As the designer, that person is me, so these are my commitments:

  • No ads, no tracking, and no selling of your stories.
  • Text comes first, so it loads on an old phone and a slow connection.
  • Every page works with a screen reader and a keyboard.
  • The neighborhood owns the server, and the neighborhood can change the rules.

The sun is up. The light stays on for the next person.
